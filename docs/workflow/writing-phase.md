# Writing Phase

The writing phase contains two workflows:

1. Flowers Cycle (`/writing-with-agents:flowers-cycle`) is the single-article inner loop. It takes a topic through the Madman, Whirlybird, Architect, Carpenter, Judge, and Quality Rubric phases.

2. Content Strategy (`/writing-with-agents:content-strategy`) is the multi-article outer loop. It takes a research corpus through Intake, Gap Analysis, Domain Mapping, Content Plan, Production, and Knowledge Harvest.

The content strategy workflow invokes the flowers cycle for each article in the content plan.
