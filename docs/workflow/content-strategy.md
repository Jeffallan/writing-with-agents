# Content strategy workflow

The content strategy workflow produces multiple articles from one research corpus:

1. Research Intake: ingest the source material and build a knowledge map.
2. Gap Analysis: find research gaps and fill them, with the human steering.
3. Domain Mapping: generate a domain whirlybird and identify the content topology.
4. Content Plan: plan the pillar, cluster, and targeted articles and their production order.
5. Production: run the Flowers cycle for each article.
6. Knowledge Harvest: capture the research artifacts back to the vault.

The outer loop runs once per domain. The inner loop (Flowers cycle) runs once per article.
