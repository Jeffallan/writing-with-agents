# Flowers cycle workflow

This workflow takes a single article through the complete Flowers writing cycle:

1. Madman: AI generates 3-5x raw material, and the human seeds it with experience.
2. Whirlybird: AI generates 2-3 mindmap options, and the human selects.
3. Architect: the human decides the structure, and AI formulates the blueprint.
4. Carpenter: AI builds prose section by section, and the human spot-checks.
5. Judge: AI runs 5 detection passes, and the human decides what to fix.
6. Quality Rubric: AI scores 10 dimensions and routes rework if needed.

Each phase has explicit handoff deliverables and human decision points.
